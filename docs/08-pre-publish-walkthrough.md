# Lesson 8 — Final Pre-Publish Walkthrough

Export day. Follow these steps in order; they take about 30 minutes
and catch basically every error that would otherwise bounce or
embarrass you. Nothing here publishes anything — we only prepare and
locally spot-check files, the way you'd study a screenshot before
uploading.

## Step 1 — Freeze the design

1. Save/save-as the working master (`.xcf`, `.afphoto`, or Canva
   project). Name it with a version: `cover-v3-final.xcf`.
2. Hide or delete the template/guides layer (if using the KDP
   template — Lesson 6).
3. Run the five-minute sweep (Lesson 7). If anything fails,
   fix it now, re-save a new version; don't edit "final."

## Step 2 — Export, then measure the export

4. Export as JPG (quality max) OR PDF Print with bleed already
   inside the art and crop marks OFF.
5. Open the exported file in any image-viewer or photo app and
   verify pixel dimensions against math:

   ```
   expected width  px = wrap width in inches  x 300
   expected height px = wrap height in inches x 300
   ```

   Example (6x9, 288-page cream): 12.97 x 9.25 → 3891 x 2775 px.
6. Zoom to 100% and pan: look at all four edges for white slivers
   (missing bleed) and letter fragments poking past guides.

## Step 3 — The "screenshot spot-checks"

Take these three screenshots (or export quick crops) and study them
like a reviewer, not a designer. This satisfies the "check like you
would a screenshot" habit while staying fully offline and
non-publishing:

7. **Front-cover crop.** Crop just the front panel (plus a sliver of
   bleed). Ask: thumbnail test (Lesson 5) still passes? Title
   centered or aligned with intent? Any amateur tells visible?
8. **Back-cover crop.** Barcode zone empty and quiet? Text block
   readable and top-aligned consistently? Palette continuous with
   the front?
9. **Spine strip crop.** (100+ page books only.) Text vertically
   centered, padded, not touching the spine-lane edges. Correct lane
   boundaries — re-measure lane boundaries against the math in
   Lesson 3.

Keep those three spot-check images in a folder with your export —
your own local review gallery. (Optional: stitch back+spine+front as
one flat "wrap preview" image for your records.)

## Step 4 — Ebook edition math

10. Ebook cover: export the *front-panel-only* design at 1600 x 2560
    px minimum (we recommend 2000 x 3125 px or larger), JPG, sRGB.
    Note: ebook cover = front cover only; no spine, no back, no
    bleed. Same text spot-checks apply at thumbnail size.

## Step 5 — Naming and file hygiene

11. Name files clearly:

    ```
    TITLE_print_wrap_6x9_288pg.jpg      (full wrap w/ bleed)
    TITLE_front_ebook_2000x3125.jpg     (ebook front)
    cover-v3-final.xcf                  (working master)
    spotchecks/front.jpg / back.jpg / spine.jpg
    ```

12. Back up the whole cover folder (external drive, private cloud).
    Masters are irreplaceable; exports are re-exportable.

## Step 6 — (When you're actually ready to upload) the manual checklist

This manual contains no publishing tools and makes no uploads — but
so you're never staring blankly at the real screen later, here's the
sequence you'll perform *there*, from your own prepared files:

13. Prepare a text file for upload day: trim size, page count, paper,
    and the spine-width number you computed; the KDP cover setup form
    will ask for these and getting them right is the difference
    between "matches" and "rejected."
14. Spot-check one last time: **file you upload = file you measured**
    (Step 2's pixel math). Same file, same folder, same name.
15. After the platform runs its own previewer (a browser-based page
    image and cover check), *screenshot its report to your folder* and
    compare the wrap it renders against your Step-3 spot-checks.

That last comparison is the most satisfying 30 seconds in cover
design: your local file, matching the platform's preview, exactly.

## The one page you can tape to your wall

```
□ master saved (version-named)
□ template/guides layer hidden
□ Lesson 7 five-minute sweep passed
□ export measured at 300 DPI against the formula
□ all four edges swept for white slivers
□ front / back / spine spot-check crops saved
□ spot-check vs. calculator template lane boundaries MATCH
□ ebook front exported (no spine/back, 1600x2560 px min)
□ clear filenames + folder backed up
```

Nine boxes. When they're all ticked, you are done — and good.

---

**The end of the manual.** Return to Lesson 5 whenever a new cover
feels off; the checklist is the tool that makes the next one faster.
