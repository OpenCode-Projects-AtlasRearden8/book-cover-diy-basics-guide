Lesson 7 — Common Amateur Tells (and the Fix for Each)

Nobody designs an amateur cover on purpose. These tells creep in one at a time. Skim the list before your first export and again after. Each tell comes with the fix — nearly all fixes take under five minutes.

The tells

  1. Default fonts. Papyrus on a fantasy cover; Comic Sans on a cozy mystery; Brush Script on a romance; naked Times New Roman anywhere. Fix: Lesson 4's font list; pick a genre-appropriate display font and one worker font.

  2. Ten visuals, zero hierarchy. Moon + horse + sword + clouds + sparkle brush + ancient rune + sunset gradient + drop shadow + vignette + quote blurbs. Fix: cut until one anchor remains; the cut stuff becomes texture only if it whispers.

  3. Text in the bleed zone. Letters near the outer 0.125" edge. Sweep: draw a rectangle band 0.125" deep all around; if any glyph intersects it, move it inward. Fix: Lesson 2's safe zones.

  4. Titles and author names at identical size. The cover reads "JOHN SMITH a tale of something" rather than the reverse. Fix: establish hierarchy in Lesson 4's order — then author is smaller or subtler, except truly-famous names cold-starting a brand.

  5. Melting letters. Distorted fonts (stretched, squashed, rotated into unreadable swoops) when the designer wanted "creative." If 20 seconds of effort cannot make a word readable, the fix is a different font, not more distortion. Fix: swap fonts; reserve heavy warp for logo-level work only.

  6. White rectangles where art should be. Cut-off images that leave a hard edge in the middle of the cover. Either extend the art (scale so it bleeds) or convert the choice into an intentional frame. Fix: scale/crop the image to run past the trim on all sides, or design a deliberate visible frame.

  7. Ribbon banners, gold filigree, and 2011-style badge work slapped on modern layouts. Date-stamps the cover instantly. Fix: delete, or restyle as a single simple line of type "as seen on" band in the shelf's current style.

  8. Rainbow palettes. Five unrelated hues plus a gradient. Fix: pick 2–3 colors + 1 accent (Lesson 1), then recolor every element into that set. Gradients allowed only inside one hue-family.

  9. Glow, everywhere. Glow used as a readability aid and decor at once gets muddy — and at thumbnail scale glow becomes mush. Fix: either a deliberately backlit title with no competing glow in the art, or no glow anywhere. One effect gets the spotlight.

  10. Quote blurbs louder than the title. "As thrilling as ... " in giant serif with quotes taking up 50% of the cover. Blurb prominence is earned by the author's name, not by applause. Fix: shrink blurb or remove it; authors without blurbs have clean covers, and this is normal.

  11. The uncropped back cover. Front got all the attention; the back layout is text soup — 300-word synopsis, no paragraph breaks, no consideration of the barcode zone at bottom-right. Fix: back cover = hook (biggest), two short paragraphs, blurb (optional, small), barcode zone left empty; keep the palette consistent with the front.

  12. Spine guessing. Any text on a thin spine, eyeballed lane boundaries, off-center text. Fix: Lesson 3's formula and guides; spine text only 100+ pages.

  13. Photo of a person, tiny in the middle of nowhere. Low resolution, flat lighting, cut-out with jaggies. Fix: either run the photo full-bleed with real atmosphere work, or restrict to silhouette-style treatments that don't demand beauty from your snapshot.

  14. Date-stamped effects. Bevel-and-emboss title from the 2000s, neon-edge glow from the sci-fi 2010s, letterpress texture wearing out its welcome. Covers date like haircuts. Fix: study current covers of your genre (context board, Lesson 5) and copy the effect-level, not the layout concept verbatim.

  15. The "close enough" export. Pixel dimensions off by a rounding, DPI at 200 instead of 300, wrong trim size chosen in the export dialog. Fix: Lesson 8's walkthrough; the last file you upload must be the file you measured twice.

The five-minute sweep

Open the exported file and just look — no edits allowed — and tick:

  • [ ] No default fonts anywhere
  • [ ] One dominant visual element
  • [ ] No text inside the outer 0.125" band
  • [ ] Hierarchy: title > author > other (sized that way)
  • [ ] Palette: 2–3 colors + accent, no extra hues
  • [ ] Back cover isn't text soup; barcode zone clean
  • [ ] Spine (if any text) inside its lane, well padded
  • [ ] Exported file's pixel dimensions match the math

Seven of eight passes moves you to Lesson 8. Any failure: fix that one thing and re-run the sweep. Ten minutes, not ten hours, of fixes.
